Choose a Legacy Contact to access your account after you die. Learn more.

A Legacy Contact needs the access key you share with them.

Add a Legacy Contact

You can add more than one Legacy Contact.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then tap Sign-In & Security.
  3. Tap Legacy Contact.

What they can see

  • Photos
  • Notes
